# 01 — Wellness Social Media Content Engine

## Purpose

Generate useful Instagram Reel concepts for a women's wellness and sustainable habits brand while maintaining clear audience targeting, structured outputs, and factual guardrails.

## Prompt

### ROLE

You are a social media strategist who specializes in women's wellness, sustainable weight management, educational content, and healthy lifestyle education.

### CONTEXT

I run a 30 Day Habits Reset program that helps women develop sustainable healthy habits. The program focuses on behaviors such as daily walking, balanced meals, understanding food labels, consistency, and developing a healthier relationship with wellness.

My audience includes busy mothers, career women, and younger women who have struggled with weight management, consistency, and body image. They don't need perfection. They need realistic strategies that fit normal life.

### TASK

Develop 5 Instagram Reel concepts designed to educate my audience, build trust, and increase saves and shares.

### REQUIREMENTS

Each Reel should focus on one practical weight-management or healthy-habit lesson.

Avoid shame, extreme dieting, exaggerated weight-loss promises, and generic motivational advice.

Make the content relatable, practical, and evidence-informed.

Do not present medical claims as established facts without sufficient evidence.

### OUTPUT

For each Reel provide:

1. Scroll-stopping hook
2. Reel concept
3. Key educational lesson
4. Three talking points
5. Suggested visual/B-roll
6. On-screen text
7. Save/share CTA
8. Soft CTA connecting the topic to my 30 Day Habits Reset

### FACTUAL GUARDRAILS

Do not invent statistics, physiological explanations, or health claims. Flag any statement that should be fact-checked before publication.

### QUALITY CONTROL

Before producing the final answer, evaluate each concept against the audience, objective, requirements, and factual guardrails above. Remove or revise any concept that does not meet them. Then provide only the final refined concepts.

## Prompt Engineering Skills Demonstrated

- Role definition
- Context engineering
- Audience targeting
- Explicit task definition
- Output formatting
- Constraint setting
- Factual guardrails
- Self-evaluation and quality control

## Business Use Case

This prompt can serve as the foundation for a reusable AI content workflow for wellness professionals who need consistent educational social content without relying on generic one-shot prompting.
